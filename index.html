1. Establishing Scannable Hierarchy

Body Copy forms the backbone of editorial interfaces. Keeping line lengths between 60 and 75 characters prevents eye fatigue while ensuring headings stand out immediately when readers skim down the page.

“Good wireframe typography communicates structure before a single brand color or photograph is introduced.”
